Livestock management

Every animal's full story, one tap away

Click any animal in the register to open a detailed profile — showing growth history, vaccination records, open health issues, and purchase cost, all in one place.

  • Filter and search across your entire herd by species, status, or location
  • Track whether animals were born in the herd or purchased, with acquisition cost
  • See open health issues flagged directly on the animal list
  • Body condition scores recorded alongside every weigh-in
  • Visual weight-over-time chart per animal
  • Tabbed drawer with Overview, Growth, Vaccinations and Health all in one view

Farm finances

Know exactly where you stand — every single day

Pasture Ledger tracks every shilling in and out. Revenue from animal sales, milk, eggs, and produce. Costs from feed, vet bills, labor, and equipment. All in one ledger.

  • Monthly revenue vs expenses bar chart — see trends at a glance
  • Auto-logs an expense when you add a purchased animal or restock feed
  • Revenue broken down by sale type (animal, milk, eggs, produce)
  • Expenses by category — vet, labor, feed, transport, equipment
  • Net profit / loss with profit margin percentage
  • Recent transactions list across sales and expenses combined

Full feature list

Everything that's included

🐾

Unlimited animal records

No caps on how many animals you register. Add cattle, goats, sheep, pigs, chickens, horses — all species in one system.

💉

Vaccination scheduler

Set due dates and receive overdue alerts automatically. Batch number and administering vet recorded per dose.

⚖️

Weight & growth charts

Plot individual animal growth curves over time with body condition scoring and gain-per-period calculation.

🏥

Health event log

Track illnesses, injuries, treatments and vet visits per animal. Open issues flagged clearly across the dashboard.

🌾

Feed stock management

Track inventory levels per feed type, set reorder alerts, record restocks and daily usage. Cost-per-kg captured for finance.

💵

Sales revenue tracking

Record animal sales, milk, eggs, wool, and other produce. Link sales to specific animals for a complete income picture.

🧾

Expense management

Log costs by category. Purchased animals and restocked feed are auto-logged — no double entry needed.

📊

Profit & loss dashboard

Monthly revenue vs expenses chart, profit margin tracking, and breakdown by revenue type and expense category.

👥

Team management

Add farm managers and workers with role-based access. Admins control who can see and do what. Accounts can be disabled without deletion.

📱

Works on any device

Fully mobile-responsive. Use it in the field on your phone, at the desk on a laptop — same experience everywhere.

🔒

No self-registration

Only admins can add users. Your farm data stays within your team — no one gets in without an invitation.

☁️

Data persists automatically

Everything saves as you go. No export button needed — your records are there every time you come back.
